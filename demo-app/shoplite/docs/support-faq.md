# ShopLite Support FAQ

**How do I apply a coupon?** Enter it in the "Promo code" box at checkout. One coupon per order.

**Why was my coupon rejected?** `SAVE20` needs a $100 subtotal and `FLAT15` needs $60.
Expired or unknown codes are rejected.

**When is shipping free?** On orders of $50 or more after discounts.

**Do you charge tax on shipping?** No.

**I think I was overcharged.** Ask for the order number, items, region and coupon used.
Compare the receipt with the pricing policy. If the numbers don't match the policy,
escalate to engineering as a possible pricing bug.

**How long do refunds take?** 3–5 business days after approval.

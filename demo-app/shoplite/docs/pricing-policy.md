# ShopLite Pricing Policy

This is the published policy customers see at checkout. Code must match it.

## Coupons
| Code | Discount | Minimum order |
|---|---|---|
| `SAVE10` | 10% off | none |
| `SAVE20` | 20% off | $100 subtotal |
| `FLAT15` | $15 off | $60 subtotal |

Only one coupon per order.

## Shipping
- **Free shipping on orders of $50 or more** (after discounts).
- Otherwise standard shipping is $7.99.

## Sales tax
- Tax is charged **on the discounted price** (after coupons), never on the pre-discount amount.
- Shipping is not taxed.
- Rates: CA 7.25%, NY 4%, TX 6.25%, OR 0%.

## Refunds for pricing errors
If a customer was overcharged because of a pricing error, support refunds the difference
once engineering confirms the bug. Support replies to the customer within one business day.
